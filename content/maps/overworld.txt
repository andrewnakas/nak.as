# ===== THE WINDWARD CRAGS (highland zone; gated by CLIMB CLAWS) =====
# Home of the SKRAL, cliff-dwelling bird-folk who roost where stone meets sky.
# This zone sits NORTH of cliff_overlook(3,0), whose entire top row is cliff (c).
# Without the climb claws those cliff tiles are solid, so the top edge can never
# be crossed and the crags are unreachable. Owning the claws makes cliff tiles
# passable, letting you scale the overlook's top wall up into crag_ascent.
#  cliff_overlook(3,0) --climb up--> crag_ascent(3,-1) --N--> skral_roost(3,-2)
#                                    crag_ascent(3,-1) --E--> wind_terrace(4,-1)
# NOTE: every screen-transition edge that should connect is OPEN (non-cliff);
# every other border stays cliff so the climb is the only way in.
